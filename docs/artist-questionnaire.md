# Message to send the artist

Hey! We're building your website and the first thing people will see is **your story**, told as they scroll.
Answer these like you're telling a friend — voice notes are fine, rough is fine. 2–4 lines each is enough.

**1. The beginning**
- What's the first tattoo or drawing you remember that made you think "I want to do this"?
- How old were you, and what were you doing with your life at that point?

**2. Learning the craft**
- Who or what taught you? (a mentor, a shop, YouTube, mistakes…)
- What was the hardest part of the early days? Any moment you nearly quit?
- The first tattoo you ever did on someone — who was it, what was it, how did it go?

**3. Your style**
- How would you describe your style in your own words?
- What do you love tattooing most? What do you say no to?
- One piece you're proudest of, and why.

**4. How you work**
- Walk me through a session from the first message to the healed tattoo, in your own steps.
- What do you do differently from other artists? (hygiene, design time, how you treat first-timers…)
- What should someone know before they come to you for their first tattoo?

**5. The person**
- Outside tattooing, what are you into? (it shapes the art — music, nature, anime, bikes, anything)
- One sentence you'd want every client to remember about you.

**6. Facts we need**
- Name you want shown, studio name, city / address
- Years of experience, roughly how many tattoos done
- Starting price (smallest piece) and how you usually price bigger work
- Working days and hours, how long a typical session is
- Instagram / WhatsApp / email for bookings

**7. Photos (send as files, not screenshots)**
- 1–2 photos of you, ideally one while tattooing
- 10–20 of your best tattoos: sharp, good light, the whole piece in frame. Healed photos are a bonus.
- For each: style (e.g. fine line, realism) and body placement
