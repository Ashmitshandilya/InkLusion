# Message to send the artist

Bhai, your website is in progress. I'm sending a preview with this. The design is ready, but the words and photos on it right now are dummy. I need the real ones from you.

Answer like you're talking to a friend. Voice notes are fine. 2-4 lines each is enough. The more real it is, the better the site sounds like you.

*A. CHECK THE PREVIEW FIRST*
1. Name shown on the site is "Shaurya". Correct? Or do you want full name / another name?
2. School is written as "Aliens Tattoo School, Mumbai". Correct name? Which year, and how long was the course?
3. Look at the black + pink space theme with the eclipse. Does it feel like you and your studio? What would you change?
4. I wrote some lines as if you said them. Tell me yes / no / change for each:
   - "Months on practice skin before I was allowed near anyone's arm."
   - "You shouldn't need a train to Delhi to get a tattoo done properly."
   - "I draw your tattoo myself."
   - "If I think an idea won't age well on that spot, I'll say so before we start."
   - "You see the drawing before the day and can ask for changes."
   - "New needle, opened in front of you."
   - "If a spot heals patchy, come back and I'll fix it." (free touch-up? within how many days?)

*B. YOUR STORY*
5. When did you first think "I want to do tattoos"? What were you doing in life then?
6. Why Aliens? What was the hardest thing there? What is the one thing they taught you that you still follow?
7. The first tattoo you did on a real person. Who, what, how did it go?
8. Why did you open in Roorkee? When did InkLusion start?
9. Why the name "InkLusion"? What does it mean to you?
10. Any time you nearly quit, or people told you this is not a real career?

*C. YOUR STYLE*
11. Describe your style in your own words.
12. What do you love doing most? (realism, portraits, fine line, cover-ups, script, religious, anime...)
13. What do you refuse to do, and why?
14. The one tattoo you are proudest of. Why that one?
15. Do you do cover-ups and scar cover? Colour work or mostly black and grey?

*D. YOUR STUDIO AND VIBE*
16. What does the studio feel like when someone walks in? Music, lights, smell, what's on the walls.
17. What kind of people come to you mostly? (students, first-timers, couples, bikers...)
18. Three words your regular clients would use for you.
19. Outside tattooing, what are you into? Music, bikes, anime, gym, nature, anything.
20. Is there any symbol, colour or thing that is "yours"? Something we should put on the site.

*E. HOW YOU WORK*
21. Walk me through it in your own steps: from the first message to the healed tattoo.
22. Hygiene: what exactly do you do? (needles, ink brand, machine, gloves, wrapping)
23. What should a first-timer know before coming? What do they always get wrong?
24. Your aftercare instructions, the way you tell clients.
25. How much advance do you take to confirm a booking? Refund or reschedule rule?

*F. FACTS FOR THE SITE*
26. Full studio address + Google Maps link.
27. Working days and timings. Walk-ins allowed or appointment only?
28. Starting price for the smallest tattoo. How do you price bigger work (per inch / per hour / per piece)?
29. Is the price negotiable? How do you want that written?
30. Years of experience, and roughly how many tattoos done.
31. Email for bookings. Should your personal Instagram also be on the site, or only the studio one?
32. Any certificates, awards, well-known clients or artists you've worked with?

*G. PHOTOS AND VIDEO (send as files / documents, not screenshots)*
33. 2-3 photos of you. One while tattooing, one looking at the camera.
34. 15-20 of your best tattoos. Sharp, good light, whole tattoo in frame. Healed ones are a bonus.
    For each one write: style + body part.
35. 3-4 photos of the studio.
36. Your certificate from Aliens (photo).
37. Any short video of you tattooing. Even a phone clip works.
38. Your logo file, if you have one.

Take your time, but A and G are the most urgent.
